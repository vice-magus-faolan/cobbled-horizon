# Broadwater: one-time historical loot migration

This is a design for a separately approved operation, **not a world scanner, automatic launch/update step or completed Broadwater recovery**. No Broadwater world or backup was inspected. Normal new worlds and future unopened loot sources do not depend on this migration. Existing [SlashLoot lifecycle limits](slashloot.md) still apply: source destruction may discard private copies, including deposited valuables; no timed replenishment or lossless uninstall is promised.

## Recommended path

Use ordinary unmodified SlashLoot with narrowly restored `LootTable` metadata on **positively verified original, physically empty, single vanilla containers only**. Start with one chest; consider barrels only after separate verification. Do not write region files, replace terrain, insert private records directly or introduce a permanent extension. An approved target list is necessary but is not an ownership detector.

The exact upstream [Handling.forBlock](https://github.com/slashdaemon/SlashLoot/blob/a565d5165e475a7520c2623a9498965d4609b244/mc-src/src/main/java/dev/blockacademy/slashloot/core/Handling.java) requires a live pending loot-table key plus allowed config and a supported container kind. A position registered only in SavedData remains vanilla without that key. The [interaction/getOrRoll path](https://github.com/slashdaemon/SlashLoot/blob/a565d5165e475a7520c2623a9498965d4609b244/mc-src/src/main/java/dev/blockacademy/slashloot/handler/ContainerInteractionHandler.java) reads table/seed, rolls a UUID-specific copy and stores it. The [unpack mixin](https://github.com/slashdaemon/SlashLoot/blob/a565d5165e475a7520c2623a9498965d4609b244/mc-src/src/main/java/dev/blockacademy/slashloot/mixin/MixinRandomizableContainer.java) cancels vanilla unpack only for eligible containers. This is a source-derived eligibility path, not proof of real-client claims or historical classification.

Official Minecraft 26.3 `RandomizableContainer.trySaveLootTable` writes `LootTable` and a nonzero `LootTableSeed`. Chest/barrel save/load uses that table branch **instead of Items**. Setting a marker on a nonempty container can hide or lose its physical items on save/load. Therefore emptiness must be inspected **before** adding the marker, never inferred from its subsequent serialized Items field. Do not accept nonempty, partial, double, replaced, unsupported or ambiguous containers. See the [dated investigation](loot-investigation.md#container-lifecycle) for original evidence; its older extension and escrow recommendations are historical, not the current design.

## Evidence alternatives

| Method | Useful evidence | Limit and disposition |
|---|---|---|
| Structure/template discovery | Generation-era structure starts/pieces, exact resource hashes, palettes, processors, transforms and table-bearing block positions identify expected sources. Include procedural structures/features separately. | Bounding boxes are not container lists. A correct expected coordinate does not prove the current chest survived. Use as report-only candidate discovery. |
| Same-seed disposable reconstruction | Match original Minecraft/mod/datapack/config versions, generator settings, dimensions, resource ordering and generation-era seed; generate only nominated regions and compare expected blocks/tables. | The current pack plus the same seed may not reproduce historical generation. Never copy donor chunks, region files or terrain. Donor output cannot establish current-container continuity. |
| Historical registration/extension | A reviewed resolver independent of live markers could support different reward-source semantics. | SavedData injection alone cannot bypass Handling; an extension would couple interaction, identity, cleanup and persistence. Not required or implemented. Reconsider only for a concrete unsupported case with separate product approval. |
| Hybrid/manual verification (recommended) | Combine exact original source metadata with historical backups/event records or a credible uninterrupted source witness; inspect current block and inventory independently. Approve a tiny list. | Missing continuity stays unresolved even if empty and inside a matching structure. Manually placing fresh rewards elsewhere would be a new reward policy, not recovery of that chest. |

Unknown original loot-table identity is a hard rejection. Missing original container seed is a separate policy decision: zero is valid but does not recover a vanished seed. [LootRoller](https://github.com/slashdaemon/SlashLoot/blob/a565d5165e475a7520c2623a9498965d4609b244/mc-src/src/main/java/dev/blockacademy/slashloot/core/LootRoller.java) uses player luck/context and [SeedDeriver](https://github.com/slashdaemon/SlashLoot/blob/a565d5165e475a7520c2623a9498965d4609b244/common/src/main/java/dev/blockacademy/slashloot/common/SeedDeriver.java) XORs the container seed with UUID bits, without position. A common zero seed reuses derived RNG seeds for the same player, risking correlated rewards. Prefer the known original seed. If unknowable, require separate approval of a fresh, recorded per-source signed 64-bit seed and label the result **new entitlement**, not original-roll restoration. Do not silently adopt a template zero as the historical placed seed. Prior looters/claims cannot be inferred from an empty chest; existing players may receive an additional entitlement.

## One-time procedure

1. **Authorize and bound.** The operator names the stopped-copy input, exact pack/runtime/resources, permitted dimensions/chunks, target count, reward/seed policy, time/disk budget and responsible executor. This document grants no live access. First run on an independently owned disposable copy with external integrations disabled and no production credentials.
2. **Establish recovery.** Take one consistent stopped-instance snapshot of the full world/all dimensions, player inventories and identities, SlashLoot state, mods and configs. Keep an off-host copy; rehearse cold restoration before any Broadwater application. Discover actual SavedData files from the copied runtime rather than assuming a literal `world/<dimension>/data/` layout. Preserve source and backup bytes untouched.
3. **Discover read-only.** Use exact generation-era templates/pieces and, where available, a bounded same-seed donor. Record candidate rationale and rejected/unknown coverage. Do not scan every empty chest or access new regions merely because the tooling can.
4. **Prove each current source.** Bind expected original source to current block continuity, independently of coordinate/emptiness. Reject a known replacement, missing witness/history, changed block/state, unsupported type, either double half, nonempty Items, existing table/seed conflict, SlashLoot block/private record or staged import conflict. Inspect actual physical slots while tableless. No commands below prove this provenance or inspect SavedData for you. If a required observation is unavailable, stop that target.
5. **Freeze the explicit manifest and dry run.** For every proposed source record: world and snapshot identity and digest; dimension and coordinate; expected block and state; original-generation resource and table identity; seed and original-versus-new policy; source and continuity evidence references; complete block-entity preimage and physical items; relevant absence of private and staged import state; proposed exact metadata; reason; operator approval identity and manifest digest. Default action is report-only. Report exclusions with reasons; do not include runnable writes for unresolved entries. Keep private coordinates/UUIDs/audits outside Git.
6. **Approve exact bytes and recheck.** Operator approves the frozen target manifest and consistent rollback point, not a generic confirmation flag. Keep players/automation out of the maintenance copy. Immediately reread the full preimages, physical slots, single state and private/import conflicts. A changed or missing preimage invalidates approval. Prevent concurrent world edits throughout; command syntax alone cannot enforce continuity, full-preimage matching or mod-state absence.
7. **Apply only metadata.** Use the existing authenticated console and native metadata commands, one approved source at a time. Do not use setblock, clear/replace Items, fill/clone or terrain transfers on the migration input. Capture command outcome and complete postimage; confirm exactly the proposed table/seed and no unrelated metadata/item changes. A refusal or no-output command is not a successful application.
8. **Save, stop and verify.** Complete all-dimension save with zero server exit; restart the disposable copy offline, inspect metadata and unchanged controls. Then separately test ordinary SlashLoot claims with distinct real identities and private remainder persistence. A new marker or successful boot alone does not prove an entitlement was generated or persisted. Keep mixed doubles excluded here and tested separately under [personal-loot acceptance](acceptance-tests.md#personal-loot).
9. **Commit audit, retire tools.** Record per-source before/after/status, manifest digest, runtime identity, executed checks, exclusions and rollback outcome. Stop the fixture and remove only its owned disposable resources. Remove temporary functions/command batches from launch paths; retain the private read-only audit. No startup hook, background scanner or production dependency remains.

### Small explicit command example

The following is **only a synthetic single-chest example**, not a Broadwater target or a complete migration tool. The read commands are the report-only default. The guarded write requires the exact manifest approval and fresh checks above. `minecraft:chests/simple_dungeon` and seed `1234567` are fixture inputs, not a general reward policy.

```mcfunction
execute in minecraft:overworld run data get block 0 100 0
execute in minecraft:overworld if block 0 100 0 minecraft:chest[type=single] unless data block 0 100 0 Items[0] unless data block 0 100 0 LootTable run data merge block 0 100 0 {LootTable:"minecraft:chests/simple_dungeon",LootTableSeed:1234567L}
execute in minecraft:overworld run data get block 0 100 0
```

The live command guards single-chest type, absence of serialized physical items while tableless, and absence of a current table. It does **not** detect player replacements, approve a target, validate table availability/blocklists, compare complete preimages, reject a stray seed or inspect SlashLoot/import state. Those are mandatory preflight exclusions. Under the maintenance lock, the validated manifest supplies those missing facts; without them this is not safe to apply. Console lines omit the leading slash.

### Idempotency, interruption and rollback

- For each approved source journal `planned`, `applied` and `verified` with full pre/postimages. Rerun is a no-op only when exact expected postimage and matching audit are present. A preexisting marker without that audit, unexpected seed, Items, changed state or private/import record is a conflict, never silently adopted.
- The guarded command does not rewrite an already marked source. If interrupted after a write but before its receipt, stop and compare the complete current metadata plus matching original manifest/backup; do not clear the marker or guess which targets succeeded. Resume only verified unchanged preimages; reconcile exact postimages into the audit after review. Partial application is not an atomic batch.
- Before any player claim/private entry, metadata-only undo is conceivable after exact postimage verification, but prefer the tested full stopped snapshot. After rewards/private inventories exist, removing LootTable alone is **not complete rollback**. Restore matching world, player and mod state together under maintenance or separately design accounting reconciliation; never delete selected SavedData rows to make a retry pass.
- Preserve failed outputs and restore a new disposable copy first. Verify source metadata, physical sentinel items, player inventories and private remainders after restart; never overwrite the only good snapshot. The [basic rollback requirement](storage-and-recovery.md#personal-loot-retention) remains distinct from accepted destruction loss.

## Proof and remaining work

See the [dated disposable metadata proof](verification.md#slashloot-263) for actual execution and limits. Synthetic tableless fixtures do not reproduce a player opening/draining a historical source and cannot establish ownership. Metadata durability does not prove per-player menu handling or claims. Real Java/Bedrock, post-claim private persistence and matching player/mod-state rollback remain pending until actually exercised.

Tracked separately in [issue #56](https://github.com/vice-magus-faolan/cobbled-horizon/issues/56); this design/proof does not complete or close that world-specific operation.

**Finite Broadwater follow-up:** obtain separately authorized consistent copies plus generation/continuity evidence; nominate one verified empty original single chest; rehearse the complete approved manifest, dry run, negatives, save/restart, two-player claim and consistent restore before considering a larger bounded list. Exclude everything unresolved. Do not make reusable integration wait for unavailable historical inputs.

Planning sizes, not measured commitments: roughly 0.5–1 engineer-day for a narrow metadata allowlist and disposable lifecycle proof once evidence exists; 1–3 days for bounded structure/template discovery; 2–5 days plus IO/generation for an exact-history same-seed donor. Missing generation archives or continuity records can make a target unverifiable, not merely slower. A permanent source extension would require separate design and scope, not an automatic next phase.
